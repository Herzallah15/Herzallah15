Hi there 👋 I'm Herzallah Alharazin, a postdoc in theoretical physics at Ruhr University Bochum.

I work on hadron and nuclear physics, and I build generative models (mostly diffusion models) for physics problems where data are sparse, noisy, or expensive to sample.

[INSPIRE](https://inspirehep.net/authors/2147029) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-4309-0793)

## Generative ML for physics

### Neutron-star matter
A denoising diffusion model that reconstructs the equation of state from sparse astrophysical observations, with a nuclear-theory prior and perturbative-QCD and astrophysical constraints enforced exactly.

Paper: [arXiv:2608.17457](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17457)

### Gravitational form factors of the proton
A diffusion model trained on synthetic curves that reconstructs $A(t)$, $J(t)$ and $D(t)$ from a few noisy data points and gives the nucleon D-term.

Paper: [Phys. Rev. D 113, 116007](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19267) · Code: [diffusion-gff-reconstruction](https://github.com/Herzallah15/diffusion-gff-reconstruction)

### Sampling lattice gauge fields
Score-based diffusion for 2D SU(2) gauge theory. Trained at a single coupling on an 8×8 lattice, it generates other couplings and lattice sizes without retraining.

Paper: [Phys. Rev. D 114, 014522](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09045) · Code: [DiffusionModelSU2](https://github.com/Herzallah15/DiffusionModelSU2)

## Lattice QCD and hadron structure

- **Correlator construction and tensor contraction tools** for multi-hadron lattice calculations: [Hadrontractions](https://github.com/Herzallah15/Hadrontractions), [PyTorTractor](https://github.com/Herzallah15/PyTorTractor)
- **Chiral EFT in an external gravitational field**: gravitational form factors of the nucleon and the Δ, and the p → Δ⁺ transition. [Papers on INSPIRE](https://inspirehep.net/authors/2147029)

## Built from first principles
